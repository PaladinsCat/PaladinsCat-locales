# Gameplay translation sources

## Scope and naming

The September 2026 gap-filling pass uses community Paladins Wiki gameplay
terminology for German, Latin American Spanish, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese
and Russian. Its import scope is short gameplay descriptions, mechanics and
terminology for abilities, cards and talents. It excludes champion summaries,
lore, strategy commentary and other narrative sections.

Use established localized proper names in those five languages. When a wiki
field is still English, an unambiguous existing native-game title may supply
the localized name. For example, native message ID `212457` supplies the name
of Tendril where the wiki has no localized field. In French, Japanese, Korean,
Turkish, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese, retain English proper
names in newly authored translations unless that policy is explicitly changed.
Translate ordinary interface and gameplay terms normally.

Canonical English catalog strings determine current mechanics, quantities and
placeholders. A stale wiki revision must not roll back those facts. Preserve
placeholder forms such as `{remaining}` and `{0.6|0.6}` exactly. Matching
quantities alone is not sufficient to establish semantic equivalence.

Existing translations and contributor-owned pending work are preserved. New
original interface wording and game-description templates were authored and
reviewed by the assistant, not generated by the local Qwen/NInfer model. This
does not constitute independent native-speaker review or Weblate approval.

## Release boundary

These additions require the normal locale review process. A translation-branch
commit does not approve Weblate suggestions, advance the frontend locale pin,
or publish translations to production.
